### Hack4Rail Feedback

- **Project Participation:** NGE and DAC Migration had challenges for Hack4Rail. Their participation was successful, and the challenges resulted in useful outputs for the projects, including open pull requests.
- **Event Outcomes:** Many participants produced working proofs of concept, which were more substantial than mockups and highly valuable.
- **Future Event Location:** There was a discussion about hosting the next Hack4Rail event in Strasbourg.
- **Event Success Factors:** Many attendees joined due to their interest in the challenges, contributing to the event's success.
- **Preparation for Next Year:** The OpenRail Association should be better prepared for the next event. If the goal is to achieve real production during the event, significant preparation will be required beforehand.
- **Award Categories:** It was suggested to introduce multiple prize categories, such as Best Presentation, Best Code, and Best Low-Cost Solution.
- **Networking:** The networking opportunities created during the event were highly beneficial.
- **Contributions:** Contributions from the event were pushed under the [OpenRail-Playground](https://github.com/orgs/OpenRail-Playground) organization.
- **Winner:** The winning project was **Taxi**, a tool designed to assist passengers during disrupted train journeys.
